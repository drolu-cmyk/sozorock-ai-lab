# Design system notes

## Creative direction

SozoRock AI Lab should feel like a civic AI research desk: maps, policy sheets, training cards, local business artifacts, public-sector diagrams, sculptural objects, and practical operating-system language.

The visual system follows the image-direction rule of eight distinct horizontal section references, one section per image, while translating the concept into a real frontend. The original guide emphasized art direction, light mode, strong imagery, composition variety, and implementation clarity.

## Palette

- Stone: `#D8CCBA`

## Copy spine

```txt
Learn / Build / Deploy / Govern
```

This phrase should stay visible in navigation, section labels, diagrams, and CTA areas.

## Voice

- Serious, but not bureaucratic
- Local-first, not hype-first
- Strategic, but practical
- Nonprofit/public-interest tone with premium design discipline

## Section map

1. Hero — AI advantage is local.
2. Need — A structured system, not another AI talk.
3. Framework — Learn. Build. Deploy. Govern.
4. Ecosystem — For the places doing the work.
5. Programs — From AI readiness to real adoption.
6. Process — Train / Build / Fund / Govern.
7. Outcomes — Communities become AI-capable.
8. CTA — Build the AI system your community actually needs.
