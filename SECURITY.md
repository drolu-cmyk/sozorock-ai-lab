# Security Policy

## Supported deployment

Security fixes are applied to the production branch (`main`) and the AWS deployment at <https://ai-lab.sozorockfoundation.org/>.

## Report a vulnerability or AI incident

Email `contact@sozorockfoundation.org` with the subject **Urgent AI Lab Security or AI Incident**.

Include:

- the affected URL or component;
- the date and time observed;
- clear reproduction steps that do not access or alter another person's information;
- the expected and actual behavior;
- the possible impact;
- a safe contact method.

Do not include passwords, authentication codes, private records, or unnecessary personal information in the initial report.

## Coordinated disclosure

Please allow a reasonable period for investigation and remediation before public disclosure. Do not use denial-of-service testing, credential attacks, social engineering, destructive techniques, mass automated scanning, or data extraction without written authorization.
