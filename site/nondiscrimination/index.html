Nondiscrimination Statement

Policy

The SozoRock Foundation Inc. prohibits discrimination in its programs, operations, employment, procurement, partnerships, application review, and program access.

Protected categories

This includes discrimination based on race, color, religion, creed, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, national origin, disability, marital status, veteran status, or any other category protected by law.

Program access

SozoRock AI Lab applicants and participants are reviewed and supported according to program criteria, mission alignment, capacity, readiness, and applicable requirements. The Foundation seeks to provide fair access to program information, application pathways, and participant communications.

Reasonable accommodation

Applicants or participants may request a reasonable accommodation related to disability, access needs, communication format, or application participation by emailing contact@sozorockfoundation.org with the subject line “AI Lab Accommodation Request.” Requests are reviewed case by case.

Concerns

Questions or concerns about discrimination, access, or accommodation may be sent to contact@sozorockfoundation.org. Please include enough information for the Foundation to review and respond.
