THE METHOD

Capability comes from a repeatable practice.

Each mission asks learners to make a decision, create evidence, test an assumption, and explain the result.

01Understand

Define the user, task, and boundary.

02Observe

Study the current process and its limits.

03Build

Create the smallest useful workflow.

04Break

Test failure modes and unsafe requests.

05Improve

Change the design based on evidence.

06Document

Record decisions, controls, and limits.

07Explain

Brief the people who must use or govern it.

SAFE LAB BOUNDARY

Capability without careless access.

The lab keeps learning useful by keeping the boundaries explicit. The full policy and setup guide live in the repository.

Read the lab-safety policy ↗
01

Use safe data. Work with synthetic, public, or properly de-identified material.

02

Stay authorized. Test only supplied targets or systems with written permission.

03

Keep a human boundary. Side effects require explicit approval and reversible steps.

04

Preserve evidence. Record the test, expected result, actual result, and reviewer decision.
