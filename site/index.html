02/ 08Build lab

A build lab for non-technical people

Bring one problem. Leave with a working system.

Participants do not sit through a generic AI talk. They bring one real problem and shape it into a practical AI-assisted workflow for daily life, work, business, education, nonprofit service, or community leadership.

No coding required
A laptop, a real problem, and a builder’s mindset are enough to start.

Examples are introduced inside the cohort, not crowded onto the page. The public promise is simple: practical AI capacity, built around real work.

03/ 08Model

The operating model

Learn. Build. Deploy. Govern.

Awareness is not enough. The Lab turns AI interest into practical use, daily operating habits, and responsible review.

05/ 08About

About SozoRock Foundation

Build the people, systems, and trust needed for the AI era.

The SozoRock Foundation Inc. advances practical capacity building for people, communities, and local institutions. SozoRock AI Lab is its educational path for local AI readiness.

Program strategy and training are led by Oluwabiyi Adeyemo, Director of Strategic Initiatives.

The Lab is educational and capacity-building content only. It does not provide legal, financial, procurement, or compliance advice, and it does not claim any institutional partnership unless formally documented.

06/ 08Opportunity

Product opportunity

From local lab to scalable AI adoption platform.

The Lab starts with in-person learning and use-case discovery. Over time, repeated needs can become templates, agents, workflow kits, and responsible-use controls for non-technical people and small organizations.

SozoRock AI Lab is a charitable capacity-building initiative. Scalable software or commercial tools may be developed through a separate for-profit entity where appropriate.

The near-term priority is trust and practical adoption. The long-term opportunity is packaging what repeats.
