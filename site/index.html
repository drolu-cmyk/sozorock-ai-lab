Practical AI skills for work and community life.

SozoRock AI Lab is a no-cost education program of The SozoRock Foundation.

We help adults and community organizations learn how to use everyday AI tools safely, critically, and productively.

No technical background is required.

Apply to participate Host a workshop

Who the Lab serves

The Lab is designed for people and organizations that want practical experience with AI but may not have access to formal technology training.

Participants may include nonprofit staff, community workers, adult learners, local organizations, and people who want to become more confident using AI in their work.

What participants learn

Training focuses on practical use.

Participants learn how to:

  • understand what current AI tools can and cannot do;
  • use AI for common work such as writing, research, meetings, planning, and information organization;
  • check AI-generated information before relying on it;
  • protect private and sensitive information;
  • recognize situations where human judgment should remain in control; and
  • develop a useful AI-assisted process for work they already perform.

The Lab is tool-neutral. Participants may encounter different AI products depending on the activity and available access.

How training works

Programs may be offered as community workshops, guided learning sessions, or cohort-based training.

Each program publishes its audience, schedule, format, and learning objectives before enrollment.

Participation is offered at no cost.

What we measure

The goal is not simply to expose people to AI.

We want to know whether participants leave better able to use these tools independently and responsibly.

Depending on the program, we may measure changes in confidence, task completion, quality, time saved, ability to verify AI output, and continued use after training.

Program results will be published as evidence becomes available.

For community organizations

Libraries, nonprofits, workforce organizations, educational institutions, local governments, and other community partners may work with the Lab to make practical AI education available to the people they serve.

Organizations can:

  • Host a workshop
  • Refer participants
  • Sponsor participant access
  • Support technology access
  • Collaborate on research and evaluation
