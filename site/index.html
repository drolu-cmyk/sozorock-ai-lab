Who the Lab serves

The Lab is designed for people and organizations that want practical experience with AI but may not have access to formal technology training.

Participants may include nonprofit staff, community workers, adult learners, local organizations, and people who want to become more confident using AI in their work.

  2. 02Community workers
  3. 03Adult learners

Learning in practice

Edward Jones brought a real business need to the AI Lab: build and deploy a website for his interior painting business, Capital Property Care.

The work moved from learning how to direct AI tools, to building and reviewing the site, to deployment with attention to security and human judgment.

What participants learn

Training focuses on practical use.

  1. 01

    understand what current AI tools can and cannot do;

  2. 02

    use AI for common work such as writing, research, meetings, planning, and information organization;

  3. 03

    check AI-generated information before relying on it;

  4. 04

    protect private and sensitive information;

  5. 05

    recognize situations where human judgment should remain in control; and

  6. 06

    develop a useful AI-assisted process for work they already perform.

The Lab is tool-neutral. Participants may encounter different AI products depending on the activity and available access.
