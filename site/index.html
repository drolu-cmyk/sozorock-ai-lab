04/ 12The operating model

The operating model

Learn. Build. Deploy. Govern.

SozoRock AI Lab helps communities move from awareness to capability, from experimentation to deployment, and from adoption to responsible governance.

Learn

Training for people, teams, and local leaders to build practical AI skills and confidence.

Build

Create practical AI tools, prototypes, and workflows that solve real problems.

Deploy

Move from experiments to daily work, service delivery, and useful operations.

Govern

Set review practices, safety habits, and responsible-use controls.

A practical build lab for non-technical people.

Product opportunity

10/ 12About

About SozoRock Foundation

Build the people, systems, and trust needed for the AI era.

The SozoRock Foundation Inc. advances practical capacity building for people, communities, and local institutions. Its work focuses on helping underserved or underprepared groups build the skills, systems, and confidence needed to participate in the AI era.

SozoRock AI Lab sits under the Foundation as an educational and capacity-building initiative. Program strategy and training are led by Oluwabiyi Adeyemo, Director of Strategic Initiatives.
