SozoRock AI Lab is a free learning program of The SozoRock Foundation. Participants learn by working on a real task with guidance, practice, and review.

Apply to the LabFor organizations

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Program format and schedule vary by cohort.

Stop starting over.

Work is often spread across notes, email, and repeated prompts. The Lab helps participants turn one task into a clear process they can use again.

→

Learn through the work.

The Lab does not begin with a list of tools. It begins with a task that matters to the participant.

Bring the work

Choose one task, decision, or recurring responsibility.

Give AI the right context

Set the goal, facts, limits, examples, and information the tool needs.

Check the result

Review sources, assumptions, privacy, accuracy, and possible harm.

Keep what works

Save the instructions and review steps so the process can be used again.
